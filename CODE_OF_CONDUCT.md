# Code of Conduct

Participants and contributors should keep this workshop respectful, constructive, and focused on learning. Be considerate of different experience levels. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Do not share confidential material or real customer data. Ask before recording or redistributing participant contributions.

Facilitators should address harmful or disruptive behaviour promptly and privately where practical. Anyone who feels unable to participate safely may contact the workshop organiser. This fictional repository does not replace an organisation's formal conduct or reporting channels.
