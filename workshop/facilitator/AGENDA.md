# Facilitator agenda

**Target:** 172 minutes (2 hours 52 minutes), including one 10-minute break. Flex within the session; do not rush human review to preserve a clock.

| Elapsed | Duration | Activity | Lab / facilitator action |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 0–5 | 5 min | Welcome and scenario | State synthetic scope, role, learning outcomes, and human ownership. |
| 5–17 | 12 min | Setup validation | Lab 00; pair participants with different IDE surfaces where useful. |
| 17–27 | 10 min | First application exploration | Lab 01; do not reveal seeded answers. |
| 27–37 | 10 min | Customisation concepts | Lab 02; use mechanism map. |
| 37–49 | 12 min | Instructions experiment | Lab 03; compare qualitatively, not as a controlled model benchmark. |
| 49–61 | 12 min | Prompt files | Lab 04; inspect and run one existing prompt, then author one small prompt. |
| 61–76 | 15 min | Specialist agents | Lab 05; require behavioural edits and boundaries. |
| 76–86 | 10 min | Break | Keep the local app and synthetic data available. |
| 86–101 | 15 min | Skill discovery and install | Lab 06; review before copy; use fallback if network or licence is unsuitable. |
| 101–116 | 15 min | Browser exploration | Lab 07; defer source until participants record an observation. |
| 116–138 | 22 min | Playwright generation/run | Lab 08; test one observation, inspect code before execution. |
| 138–155 | 17 min | Investigation and evidence | Lab 09; require classification and human decision before any app change. |
| 155–167 | 12 min | Transfer plan | Lab 10; use non-sensitive customer context only. |
| 167–172 | 5 min | Close and feedback | Use [feedback questions](FEEDBACK.md); collect decisions and follow-up. |

## Condensed demonstration (60 minutes)

For a presenter-led session, the [seven-minute concept refresher](../presentation/presentation.md) can replace part of the framing and customisation explanation. Keep it within the hour rather than adding it to the timings below, and preserve the browser-to-evidence demonstration and human review.

| Duration | Activity |
|---:|---|
| 4 min | Frame the scenario and safety boundary. |
| 6 min | Run readiness check; use a prepared app/browser if setup is blocked. |
| 6 min | Explore one notice journey as a black box. |
| 8 min | Show the instruction before/after comparison. |
| 7 min | Run an existing prompt and inspect one specialist agent. |
| 5 min | Demonstrate external skill review and the local fallback. |
| 6 min | Browser observation and one reproducible finding. |
| 10 min | Generate and execute a focused Playwright test; show screenshot/trace/report. |
| 5 min | Human classification, evidence, and close. |
| 3 min | Feedback and next step. |

For 45 minutes, omit the skill demonstration and reduce the application exploration and discussion. Do not remove the human review gate.

## Self-paced route

Ask participants to complete Labs 00–10 in order, record progress, and stop at every human-decision checkpoint. The estimated times exclude local setup delays and reflection.
